# 14 Final feasibility and research verdict

Project: hybrid image manipulation detection, localisation and adaptive competitive forensic reasoning (CFR), built and tested as a standalone project.
Training and model selection use COCO-Forge: 4000 images (384 px) made from 2000 COCO val2017 photographs with synthetic splice, copy-move and removal edits,
source-disjoint splits. A separate 1200-image set of AI-edited, AI-generated and real photographs (OpenSDI slice) is used for zero-shot testing only (`04` addendum).
Three seeds per variant, 42 training runs in total (27 headline, 15 added in stage 3). The comparison is a pilot (`12`), not a definitive evaluation.

## Verdicts, separately

| dimension | verdict | basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Engineering feasibility | **End-to-end working** | image file to label, probability, mask, overlay and JSON runs on the real checkpoint (`10`); 26 software tests pass; bad inputs are rejected with messages; application output equals evaluation output (no label changes on 500 test images). Two defects found by the end-to-end work were fixed (truncated JPEGs, padding seams). Command line and Python only; JPEG and PNG, 128 to 4096 px. |
| 2. Data feasibility | **Partially validated** | the training data are clean (no leakage, aligned masks, shortcut baselines near chance, `04`) and support claims about conventional edits of the kinds produced. AI-edited and AI-generated images exist here only as a small zero-shot test set with a weak file-size cue and one real-image source; that is enough to measure that the models fail on them, not enough to train or evaluate a detector for them. |
| 3. Model feasibility | **Technically working, research benefit unproven** | classification, localisation, alignment and fusion all work on held-out sources of the trained edit types (A: 0.994 AUROC, Dice 0.66; learned fusion 0.993; B alone 0.911). No fusion beats Model A, and the fusions learn to ignore B. Unseen removal and AI-edited or AI-generated images are not handled. |
| 4. Research validity (does adaptive CFR add value over relevant controls) | **Inconclusive, leaning unsupported** | the pre-declared rule is not met: ahead of mutual learning in all seeds with an interval that includes zero, behind mutual learning plus hard-example mining in all seeds with an interval that includes zero. Also no better than a sudden or fixed switch, no better than competition without alignment, and below mutual learning at a smaller alignment weight, below learned fusion, and below Model A alone (interval excludes zero). |
| 5. FYP feasibility | **Partially validated** | the system, data tooling, tests and evaluation protocol are complete and can be defended as an engineering and evaluation project with a carefully argued negative result. A positive research claim cannot be defended on the present evidence. |

No single percentage is given, as the brief asks.

## The ten questions

1. **Does the entire project work from image input to final output?** Yes for supported inputs on the one real checkpoint tested (`runs/cfr_adaptive_s0`). It flags splice and copy-move correctly, tiles large images, and rejects unsupported input with a message. It misses unseen removal, a 2 percent region, and the AI-edited and AI-generated examples tried.
2. **Which components have actually been executed and verified?** Data build and audit (including the external set), SRM/Bayar/DCT features, both branches and decoders, learned concatenation and gated fusion, all loss terms (unit-tested), the controller (unit-tested and run), 42 training runs, alignment-strength sweep, calibration, selective prediction, degradation tests, zero-shot AI-image tests, the inference path and its comparison with evaluation. **Not executed:** a jointly trained parameter-matched control (the compute-matched control is a post-hoc two-Model-A ensemble), any user interface beyond the command line, a literature check for novelty.
3. **Does Model B remain aligned with Model A during CFR?** Yes, without collapse: logit correlation 0.84, about 17 percent disagreement, alignment loss nonzero by construction. B's own AUROC (0.910) is the same as trained alone (0.911), so alignment kept B close to A without making it better. B does not fully avoid A's errors: it corrects 31 percent of A's misses under `cfr_adaptive` against 42 percent unaligned.
4. **Does adaptive CFR improve on mutual learning and hard-example mining?** Not shown. See dimension 4. A mutual-learning control at alignment weight 0.25 is ahead of it by 0.0034 (not bootstrapped), and competition without alignment is nominally ahead of competition with it.
5. **Does the complete system generalise to held-out data?** To held-out sources of the same edit types, yes (AUROC about 0.98). To an unseen manipulation tool, weakly (removal 0.71 to 0.73; A alone 0.795). To AI-edited and AI-generated images, no (zero-shot AUROC 0.55 and 0.59). To degradation, partly: JPEG and mild resizing cost a few points, blur breaks it.
6. **Are the localisation and confidence outputs reliable enough to report?** Localisation: report it with its number (Dice about 0.6 on test overall, about 0.9 on easy spliced cases), as moderate on seen tools and absent on unseen ones (Dice 0.01 to 0.04 on AI-edited masks). Confidence: usable as a ranking on seen tools (withholding the least confident 20 percent raises accuracy by 2 to 5 points) but not a calibrated probability on unseen manipulations; the validation-fitted temperature made calibration worse; branch disagreement adds nothing over confidence.
7. **What are the remaining blockers?** No training data for AI-edited or AI-generated images (the network gave about 100 KB/s); no benchmark where Model A is below ceiling; the blur weakness; the max-over-windows false-positive inflation on larger images; no calibration set matching deployment; whether Model A uses semantic cues rather than forensic traces is untested.
8. **What should be retained, simplified, redesigned, or removed?** *Retain:* the data tooling and audits, the evaluation protocol with the pre-declared rule, Model A, the inference tool. *Simplify:* replace the fixed-average fusion with Model A alone or the learned concatenation head; drop or simplify the adaptive controller (triggered later than a fixed epoch and was not better); drop the alignment term unless a stronger case is found (weight 4 is harmful, 0.25 is level with none). *Redesign:* Model B and its role: it is weaker, ignored by every learned fusion and not complementary here. *Remove or defer:* the claim that CFR beats mutual learning or hard-example mining.
9. **What can be confidently demonstrated in the final-year project?** A working, tested pipeline and a careful negative-to-inconclusive result: on a clean benchmark with source-disjoint splits, a visual model alone is a strong detector for conventional edits; the forensic branch adds nothing in fusion (shown with four fusion schemes and branch-reliance tests); mutual learning keeps the branches aligned but does not improve them, and strong alignment hurts; the CFR objective as specified is indistinguishable from simple hard-example weighting at this scale; and a detector trained on conventional edits does not transfer to AI-edited or AI-generated images. That result is honest and defensible; it is not a result that the method works.
10. **What evidence is still required before claiming research novelty?** (a) A benchmark where Model A is not near ceiling and where forensic traces matter, with real unseen manipulation tools and AI-edited training data. (b) Enough seeds (roughly ten or more) to resolve effects of 0.003 AUROC, or a bigger effect. (c) A jointly trained, parameter-matched control. (d) A tuned mutual-learning control (weight about 0.25) and a check that CFR beats `ml_hem` on the same data. (e) A literature check that this objective is not already known under another name; none was done. (f) Separate ablations of the challenge term and of the gated alignment.

## Single most important next action

Build or obtain a benchmark where Model A alone is clearly not at ceiling, and which includes AI-edited images for training and testing (unseen tools, real-world processing). Then rerun `single_a`, the tuned mutual-learning control, `ml_hem` and `cfr_adaptive` on it with at least ten seeds under the same pre-declared rule. Until that exists, no result on this benchmark can distinguish CFR from hard-example mining.
